Documents on your phone

Folivo stores PDFs, names, categories, favourites, thumbnails and recognised text in the app’s local storage. Text recognition (English, French and Arabic) runs on the device. We do not operate a document upload server. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes local documents. Export or back up important files first.

Optional Google Drive backup

If you enable backup and authorize Google access, PDFs stored in the app are uploaded to your Google Drive in the folder you choose in the app (“scanned documents” unless you change it). The app requests access to Drive files it creates or that you explicitly make available to it, rather than unrestricted access to your entire Drive. Google processes the uploaded files under your Google account and its policies. Google authorization tokens are used for Drive requests; this app does not deliberately log them or store them as document metadata.

Your controls and retention

You can delete local documents, export or share them, and pause automatic backup in Settings. Pausing does not remove existing copies. Local deletion does not remove a Drive backup; delete remote copies in Drive. Revoke authorization through your Google account settings. Local document metadata remains until you delete it or clear the app; cached exports may remain until the app cache is cleared. Text copied to the clipboard is managed by your device. Recipients and other apps control copies you share with them.

Google services and diagnostics

The app uses Google Play services, ML Kit and Google Play Billing. These services may process app/device identifiers, diagnostics and usage information for their services. Review Google’s ML Kit disclosure and Google’s privacy policy. No separate developer analytics service is included.

Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob, and offers optional short videos that unlock Pro tools for a limited time. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device’s advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, app interactions and device information. Folivo never sends your documents, their text or their names to advertisers. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads, and you can change your choice at any time in the app’s settings (Privacy options). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone’s Android settings. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services. Buying Folivo Pro, where it is offered, removes ads.

Purchases

Google Play processes payments. The app does not receive your card details. Purchase records, tokens and signatures are used to verify and restore Pro access and retained locally while needed for that access. Google retains transaction records under its own policies. No developer purchase-verification server is used; if one is introduced, this policy will be updated.

Support and requests

If you contact us, we use the information you provide to answer your request. Messages are kept in the email inbox of the operator only as long as needed to answer you, and are deleted on request. Contact abdelkrim.amr@gmail.com for privacy questions or requests. We cannot delete files you retained or shared through your own Drive account or other apps; you manage those copies in the relevant service.
